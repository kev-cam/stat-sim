# AES-128 S-box cost characterization — self-timed static-CMOS FSM campaign

**Node:** IHP SG13G2 130 nm / `sg13g2_stdcell_typ_1p20V_25C`. **Date:** 2026-10-02.
**Lane:** the S-box — the *distinctive* element of AES vs SHA-256 (substitution, not
arithmetic). 16 S-boxes/round × 10 = **160 data S-box evals** (+40 in key expansion);
the round critical path and per-op energy are **S-box-dominated**.
**Owned files:** `sbox_cost.py`, `SBOX_ANALYSIS.md` (this). Nothing else touched.
**Reproduce:** `python3 sbox_cost.py` (add `--workdir DIR` to keep the generated Verilog
+ yosys/STA logs; by default they go to a temp dir so `aes_fsm/` stays at these two files).

Every figure is tagged **[MEAS-anchor]** (measured on transistors, prior campaign),
**[COMPOSED]** (derived here — real yosys synth + OpenSTA, scaled by a [MEAS] anchor), or
**[EST]** (honest structural estimate). Untagged numbers are definitions/counts.
**No SPICE** was launched (USER DIRECTIVE 2026-10-02: stat-sim modeling over SPICE).

| form | cells (SG13G2) | area µm² | depth (levels) | max path ns | E/eval fJ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) LUT / ROM** (256×8, decoder tree) | **410** | 4108.8 | **7** | **1.076** | **1510** |

**The trade, measured:** the composite form is **2.25× fewer cells**, **1.97× less area**,
**2.25× less energy/eval** — but **2.4× deeper** (17 vs 7 logic levels) and **3.2× slower**
path. Classic **ROM = fast/big, composite = compact/deep**. For a *low-power, idle-regime*
cipher (the whole point of the self-timed FSM), the composite form's energy/area win is the
one that matters; its extra depth just lengthens the in-kind replica-done delay (still
bounded, no ripple hazard — §5).

## 2. Form (a) — 256×8 LUT / ROM (decoder tree)

Module `aes_sbox` (RTL agent's FIPS-197 `case`). SG13G2 has no ROM primitive, so yosys
realizes it as **minimized two-level-ish random logic** — the "decoder tree" form.

- **410 cells, 4108.78 µm², 7 logic levels, 1.076 ns** max comb path [COMPOSED/STA].
- Cell mix: **AOI/OAI-dominated** — `o21ai`×69, `a21oi`×68, `a221oi`×32, `a22oi`×22,
  `nand2`×40, `nor2`×37, `mux4`×5, only 7 XOR/XNOR. (A minimized truth table, not a
  one-hot decoder → ~random-logic activity, so the per-cell energy basis applies.)
- **Shallow (7 levels)** → the fast/sharp form; the price is **2.25× the cell/area/energy**.

## 3. Form (b) — composite-field GF((2⁴)²) tower (Canright-style)

Generated by `sbox_cost.py`: `S(x) = M_out · inv_tower(M_in · x) ⊕ 0x63`, where `M_in`,
`M_out` are 8×8 GF(2) basis-change maps (the affine transform **merged** into `M_out`), and
`inv_tower` is the multiplicative inverse built down the tower
**GF(2⁸) → GF((2⁴)²) → GF((2²)²)**. Tower found by search and verified bit-exact:
**x² + x + Φ over GF(2²) with Φ={10}; x² + x + Λ over GF(2⁴) with Λ={1000}; β=0x41** (the
image of the AES field generator). Inverse via the standard norm decomposition
(δ = Λ·a_hi² ⊕ a_lo·a_hi ⊕ a_lo², then inv_hi = a_hi·δ⁻¹, inv_lo = (a_hi⊕a_lo)·δ⁻¹),
recursed into GF(2⁴) and GF(2²) (where inverse = square).

- **182 cells, 2081.00 µm², 17 logic levels, 3.421 ns** max comb path [COMPOSED/STA].
- Cell mix: **XOR-dominated** — `xnor2`×59 + `xor2`×21 = **80 XOR (44 % of cells)**,
  `nand2`×33, `nor2`×16, `mux2`×8. The signature of a GF-arithmetic cone.
- **Compact (2.25× fewer cells) but deep (17 levels)** → the low-power/area form.

> **On the "~110–130 gate" literature figure:** Canright's compact S-box is quoted in a
> *minimal normal-basis gate count* (NAND/XOR in a specific basis, hand-shared subfactors).
> This lane reports **mapped SG13G2 standard cells** from a *polynomial-basis* tower through
> generic yosys+abc — 182 cells, no hand factor-sharing and no normal-basis optimization.
> The two counts are not the same unit; 182 SG13G2 cells is the honest as-synthesized
> figure and already lands 2.25× under the LUT. A normal-basis + shared-factor version
> would shrink it further (toward the literature count) — a known lever, not claimed here.

## 6. Round critical-path depth → self-timed replica-done, and the contrast with SHA

Round path = **S-box depth + MixColumns depth** (SubBytes → MixColumns in series):

| round form | levels | ns [COMPOSED/STA] |
|---|---|---|
| LUT S-box + MixColumns | **10** | **1.52** |
| composite S-box + MixColumns | **20** | **3.86** |
| *SHA-256 round (reference)* | — | *5.64 (32 b 5-operand adder ripple)* |

**The AES round is XOR/substitution-shallow — there is no deep ripple-carry.** MixColumns is
only **3 levels / 0.44 ns** (GF ×2/×3 is a 1-bit conditional XOR, not an adder). So the round
settle is **bounded and uniform**:
- **LUT round (1.52 ns)** is **3.7× shallower than SHA's 5.64 ns carry chain** — a **sharp,
  early power-settle edge**, ideal for current-sense / in-kind-replica completion: the "done"
  is easy to place and tight.
- **Composite round (3.86 ns)** is deeper (the nested GF inversion) but still **1.5× shorter
  than SHA and structurally balanced** (a logic cone, not one long carry chain) — the settle
  is cleaner than SHA's even when longer, so the replica-done stays well-behaved.

**Net for the self-timed FSM:** AES sheds SHA's worst completion-detection hazard (the long,
data-dependent carry ripple). The in-kind replica delay is a short static buffer matched to a
**fixed, shallow** S-box+MixColumns cone — the sharper `done` the campaign wanted. Pick the
**LUT** form when the sharpest/fastest done matters; pick the **composite** form (2.25×
lower energy/area, the idle-regime priority) when a 3.86 ns replica is acceptable.

## 7. Caveats / provenance

- Absolute pJ is **[COMPOSED]** from the measured `sha_slice` per-cell anchor × real
  yosys cell counts; no AES SPICE was run (per directive). Cell counts, area, and depth are
  **real synthesis/STA output** this session, not estimates.
- `E = cells × per-cell` assumes ~uniform per-cell activity at the anchor's α≈0.48. Both
  forms are **random logic** (the LUT is a minimized AOI network, not a one-hot ROM; the
  composite is an XOR cone), so the basis is apples-to-apples between them. α sensitivity
  bracketed in §5.
- The 182-cell composite is a **polynomial-basis, generically-synthesized** figure; a
  normal-basis / hand-shared Canright build would be smaller (§3 note) — the win over the LUT
  is therefore a **lower bound**.
- Depth is yosys `ltp` logic **levels** cross-checked with OpenSTA **ns**; both agree at
  ~0.15–0.20 ns/level for SG13G2 @ 1.2 V.

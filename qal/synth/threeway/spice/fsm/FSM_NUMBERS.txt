FSM_NUMBERS.txt — self-timed static-CMOS accumulator vs clocked golden (130nm)
Durable measured record. SG13G2/PSP103, Xyce. The reframe test (workflow
w2ltp7i1q): a self-timed static-CMOS FSM holds state + dissipates only on
transitions -> sheds the clock floor at low duty = the duty-shaped lower-power win.
Fixture: 4-bit accumulator s<-s+x. Static ripple adder (xor2/and2/or2 stdcells,
PRECHARGE-FREE, not domino) + edge-triggered dfrbp DFF register. Two decks share
the datapath; differ only in capture: golden=free-running clock (idle cycles
data-gated x=0, clock still toggles); self-timed=done-pulsed capture (in-kind
replica delay capgen). gen_fsm.py / analyze_fsm.py / run_fsm2.sh.

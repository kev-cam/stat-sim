This settles the well-rail question WITHOUT a simulation, and more strongly than
a measurement could: it is a statement about what is connected to what.

NODE vhi -- complete list of every element that touches it:
   VHI  vhi 0 1.5                                            <- the ideal source
   XSWP1 sw1 gtp1 rail1 vhi   (D G S B)  -> BULK only
   XSWP2 sw2 gtp2 rail2 vhi   (D G S B)  -> BULK only
   XSWP3 sw3 gtp3 rail3 vhi   (D G S B)  -> BULK only
   ...and NOTHING else.

CONSEQUENCE.  The only path by which charge can reach vhi is a switch-pMOS bulk
junction.  That device's other terminals are rail{k} (source), sw{k} (drain) and
gtp{k} (gate).  rail{k} and sw{k} are both ON THE TANK'S OWN CHARGE PATH; gtp{k}
is the ideal gate PWL and is metered separately by the egt integrator.

Therefore every joule the 1.5 V well supply ABSORBS (E_wellrail is measured
NEGATIVE = the supply receives) had already left the tank, and is therefore
ALREADY INSIDE the MEASURED tank droop 0.5*C_tank*(V0^2 - V1^2) that the ledger
charges as E_tank_loss.  Adding |E_wellrail| as a further cost counts the same
joules TWICE.

So the record's own sensitivity -- "charging the pMOS well rail puts N=64 at
4.711 fJ/gate = 1.138x and ERASES THE CROSSING" -- is the WRONG SIGN of
correction.  This one favours the record.
